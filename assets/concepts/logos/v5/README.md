## Lettering and Palette

The wordmark spells **Dragon Database** in Title Case using original editable paths. Uneven baselines, heavy black shapes, angular counters, and pointed medieval terminals combine the requested playful fantasy mood with a dragon character. A horizontal sword underlines the two words. Each concept uses slightly different letter angles within the same lettering family.

All initials and wordmark letters are original editable paths with no font dependency.

The primary colors are black `#101115`, silver `#C5CBD3`, and pale silver `#E4E7EB`. The silver backgrounds use a simple editable gradient that also includes cool silver `#B9C1CC`. The mark colors remain flat.

## Files and Editing

Full logo SVGs use a `960 × 360` viewBox with the requested silver background. Their PNG exports are `1920 × 720`. The four standalone mark SVGs have transparent surrounding canvases and a `360 × 360` viewBox; their PNG exports are `1024 × 1024`. The standalone wordmark PNG is `1644 × 930` on silver. PNG files share their corresponding SVG names.

Each SVG has an accessible title and description and descriptive group IDs. Keep Swordmaw's internal definitions and mask with its mark group when moving the artwork: the mask preserves open letter counters through the crossed swords. The comparison sheet has an opaque presentation background.

All four prior rounds are preserved. No application assets or code were changed, no mockups were created, and no tests, builds, or application UI checks were run.
