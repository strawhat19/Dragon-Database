Dragon Database / Design Studies

Search in Steel / Dragon Database

The selected Search in Steel direction now leads with Dragon Database in original DragonSlapper lettering: one line on desktop and stacked on mobile. The Scaling Collection subheading adds prominent scales to its S and one slit-pupil eye inside the circular bowl of its g; Collection keeps plain o letters. Angular black flames, larger flying dragons, and title-and-symbol dragon cards frame the search and Browse controls within steel. The Classic header logo and black Sign In remain preserved. This is static review artwork.
