# Swordmaw Hybrid — v7

This round combines the selected features of v6 Swordmaw Prime and Swordmaw Regalia: Prime's dragon, wings, angry face, and DB monogram; Regalia's ceremonial sword details; Prime's wider blades; and Regalia's Metal Mania lettering. Black artwork sits on silver backgrounds. Previous rounds remain intact.

## Wordmark and Typography

Both stacked word lines, “Dragon” and “Database,” have the same visible width as the complete horizontal sword underneath them. That span is 517 source units, from x = 401 to x = 918. The original Metal Mania glyph proportions are preserved with uniform scaling and custom letter spacing.

The comparison sheet includes alignment guides in its detail panel. The standalone logo files omit those guides.

[Aligned wordmark SVG](04-aligned-metal-mania-wordmark.svg) · [Aligned wordmark PNG](04-aligned-metal-mania-wordmark.png)

Metal Mania Regular 400 is the matching brand/title font. Alegreya Sans 400, 500, and 700 provides body copy and controls. Font files, licenses, and opt-in SCSS are included for later adoption across the app.

## Palette and Uses

| Color | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Black | `#101115` | Dragon silhouette, monogram, lettering, sword outlines |
| Silver | `#C5CBD3` | Sword faces and highlights |
| Pale silver | `#E4E7EB` | Blade facets and bright details |
| Silver background | `#B9C1CC` | Logo and wordmark presentation background |

Use the complete lockups for wide headers and brand panels; the transparent marks for icons and compact placements; and the separate wordmark for title treatments. SVGs contain accessible titles and descriptions. All lettering in artwork is outlined, so the previews do not depend on installed fonts.
