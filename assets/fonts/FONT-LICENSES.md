# Font License Audit

Reviewed October 5, 2026. This audit checks the exact supplied font listings and their linked author/license sources for free commercial website and app font embedding. Finished logo artwork and distributing a live font in an app require different rights.

| Font / listed style | Author | Free commercial web/app embedding | Saved font path |

## Dragon Fire

The [exact 1001Fonts listing](https://www.1001fonts.com/dragon-fire-font.html) identifies Girinesia Studio and lists Dragon Fire Regular in TTF/OTF formats. Its linked author [READ ME.txt](https://st.1001fonts.net/license/dragon-fire/READ%20ME.txt) restricts the free download to personal projects and directs commercial users to buy a license. That free grant does not cover a commercial app or company logo.

The author's linked [Creative Fabrica product](https://www.creativefabrica.com/product/dragon-fire/) presents a subscription/trial download with a commercial license. However, [Creative Fabrica's official embedding guidance](https://help.creativefabrica.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029518531-How-do-I-convert-an-OTF-or-TTF-font-to-a-Webfont) says standard Subscription and Single Sales licenses exclude embedding fonts in websites or apps and require a separate Enterprise license.

Creative Fabrica's [Subscription License](https://www.creativefabrica.com/subscription-license/) covers client logo creation while explicitly excluding font embedding. Assessment: a licensed finished logo may be possible through that route, but a commercial-license badge or free-trial offer does not establish free web/app embedding rights. No font file was saved.

## Blast Dragon

The [exact 1001Fonts listing](https://www.1001fonts.com/blast-dragon-font.html) identifies Sharkshock, lists Blast Dragon Regular, and describes the free file as a limited demo. It links the [1001Fonts Free For Personal Use License](https://www.1001fonts.com/licenses/ffp.html), which requires written author permission for commercial use. Its app embedding permission applies only to personal-use applications.

Sharkshock's [official licensing page and EULA](https://www.sharkshock.net/license.html) offers separate paid categories for desktop/logo use, CSS webfont use, and application/software use. A desktop license for a finished logo should not be treated as permission to embed the font in the app or website. The page also restricts source-font redistribution. No demo or production font file was saved.

## Dragon Slapper — Use the Original FontStruct Release

The [supplied 1001Fonts listing](https://www.1001fonts.com/dragonslapper-font.html) says free personal use and excludes free commercial use. Its linked [archive license](https://st.1001fonts.net/license/dragonslapper/license.txt) is older donationware; the designer's [current page](https://nalgames.com/fonts/dragonslapper) points commercial users to a paid license. Those terms are not the source for the font saved here.

The same designer, Allison James (NAL), published an explicitly licensed [original FontStruct release](https://www.fontstruct.com/fontstructions/show/830154/dragonslapper). Its [download agreement](https://www.fontstruct.com/fontstructions/download/830154), original archive license, and embedded TTF license records all identify **CC BY-SA 3.0**. The download page also describes a webfont format for webpages and web apps. [The CC BY-SA 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/) permits commercial use and redistribution with attribution, a license link, applicable share-alike terms, and no additional restrictions. These freedoms remain available while the license conditions are followed.

Saved the exact original FontStruct TTF and archive with the original notices, attribution, source-page evidence, and provenance in [dragon-slapper-fontstruct/](dragon-slapper-fontstruct/README.md). Use that binary, not the separately licensed 1001Fonts package. Embedded family: `DragonSlapper`; style: `Regular`. The file is unchanged. Font adaptations must retain applicable share-alike licensing and indicate changes; font attribution and licensing remain with the asset. See [ATTRIBUTION.md](dragon-slapper-fontstruct/ATTRIBUTION.md).

## Dragon Hunter

The [supplied listing and author terms](https://www.1001fonts.com/dragon-hunter-font.html) limit the free demo to personal use and expressly exclude promotional and commercial use. The [official product page](https://maknastudio.com/product/dragon-hunter/) sells commercial licensing. Makna Studio's [license FAQ](https://maknastudio.com/about/) distinguishes standard logo use, webfont embedding, and app/game/server embedding. A standard logo license is not an app-font license. No free commercial app grant was found and no demo font was saved or used.

The saved DragonSlapper source release is the only newly downloaded font. No fonts were installed, no bundled code was executed, and no application changes, tests, builds, or UI checks were performed.
