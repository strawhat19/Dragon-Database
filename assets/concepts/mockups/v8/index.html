The Scaling Collection / Steel Heroes

Three full-bleed compositions place The Scaling Collection directly inside the steel banner: a centered title, an emblem beside the title, and an integrated search hero. Curling black flames, subtle flying dragons, scale details, and slit-pupil eyes preserve the selected visual direction. The Classic header logo, black Sign In, and public dragon cards continue across these static studies.
